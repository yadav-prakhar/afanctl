## Change 1 — `src/config.rs`: the `MAX_INTERVAL_S` doc comment under-counts the worst case

The comment (around `src/config.rs:39-49`) cites "≈ 2.7 s per failing `write_speed` … ≈ 1.8 s per
failing `set_mode`" and concludes "a 12 s interval plus its own blocking keeps ≥ 3 s of margin". That
is the *single-window* figure. T9d recomputed the true in-poll ceiling from `src/smc.rs` (window
1500 ms / 9 × 150 ms sleeps / `WRITE_RETRY_MAX = 1`; mode window 1000 ms):

- common failure ceiling: failing `write_speed` ≈ 3.0 s **plus** a failing L1 mode re-assert ≈ 2.0 s
  ⇒ ≈ 5 s in one poll;
- all four write paths failing in one poll (`apply_mode`'s `set_mode`, `act`'s `write_speed`, L1's
  mode re-assert, L1's tracking re-assert) ⇒ ≈ 10 s.

The constant stays **12** (correct and safe) — because the two-ping scheme bounds the
watchdog-observed gap by `max(interval, in-poll work) ≤ 12 s`. Only the printed reasoning changes:
state the real ceilings above, and that the two pings (start and end of `step_once`) are what keep
the observed gap bounded by `max(interval, poll_work)`.
